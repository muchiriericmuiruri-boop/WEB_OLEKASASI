About Olekasasi

Olekasasi Mixed Day Secondary School, located in Ongata Rongai, is known for its commitment to academic excellence, character development, and extracurricular achievement. Our motto, "Strive To Excel," reflects our mission to help every student reach their full potential.

Our Vision

To be a leading secondary school recognized for academic excellence, strong discipline, and holistic development of students.

Our Mission

To provide quality education, nurture talents, and instill moral values that prepare students for success in life and society.
